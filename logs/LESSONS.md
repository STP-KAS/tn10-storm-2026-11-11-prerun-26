1. **Our prediction was wrong in the direction that matters.** We wrote before the run that with the lane on at 1× our floor hops would fall to well under half of BASE and mint accepts would stretch to many seconds. They did not: hops fell about 6 percent (649.7 → 611.7 a minute), every minute stayed at 98.7–101.3 percent landed of submit-OK, all 8 families attempting, mints stayed near 1 s (max 2,044 ms over the run, rule line 10 s). The lane's ready mass was about 1.3 million (2.6 blocks), not the 2.1 million we had assumed, and the template carried on average 0.88 lane transactions next to about 188,000 mass of the rest. In this run's numbers, our 8 chained families never had more than a few hops ready at once, so they fit beside one lane transaction most of the time.
2. **The 2× step moved the template, not our landings.** With the mix at 920, the template's lane share halved (0.88 → 0.47 lane transactions, 235,555 → 124,444 mass) and the rest rose (187,632 → 241,907), but floor hops only went from 611.7 to 623.3 a minute and our lane still landed 464 in two whole minutes (468 at 1×): the lane is held by its ready cap of 8, so it lands whenever a slot frees. The node's own fee estimate tracked the lane, not our mix: priority 100 at baseline, 210 with the lane, 297 with the mix at 920, back to 100 within the zero segment.
